# Cinematic.AI: User & Director Guide
**Version**: 5.0 | **Studio Edition**

Welcome to the **Cinematic.AI Unified Studio**. This guide covers the core workflows for professional AI filmmaking, from initial concept to post-production handover.

---

## 1. The Production Workflow

### A. Pre-Production: The Visual Concept Engine
1.  Navigate to the **Concepts** view (Map icon).
2.  Use the **Visual Concept Engine** to generate moodboards and visual DNA.
3.  Define the lighting, textures, and color palettes that will govern your project.

### B. The Character Bible & Forge
1.  Navigate to **Character Forge** (Users icon).
2.  Create a **Master Identity**: This locks the facial seed and core traits.
3.  Create **Outfits**: Generate variants like "Tactical Gear" or "Formal Wear" that preserve the Master's facial consistency.
4.  Reference these characters in prompts using the `@` mention system.

### C. Auto-Storyboarding
1.  Navigate to the **Project** view (Timeline icon).
2.  Click **AUTO-STORYBOARD** in the timeline header.
3.  Paste your screenplay or scene description into the engine.
4.  The AI will break down the text into a sequence of technical shots (Medium Shot, Close Up, etc.) and add them to your timeline.

---

## 2. Directorial Controls (The Sequencer)

### NLE Keyboard Shortcuts
Cinematic.AI follows industry-standard Non-Linear Editor (NLE) shortcuts:
- **[K]**: Play / Pause
- **[J]**: Rewind / Scrub Left
- **[L]**: Fast Forward / Scrub Right
- **[Space]**: Toggle Playback

### The "@" Mention System
When writing shot prompts, use the `@` symbol to trigger the **Smart Tagging** system:
- **Characters**: `@Sarah`, `@Elias` (Injects identity seeds and traits).
- **Camera Actions**: `@DollyIn`, `@Wide`, `@LowAngle` (Injects technical optics).

### Multi-Take Management
- Every shot supports multiple **Takes**.
- Render variations of a shot, rate them (1-5 stars), and "Approve" the best one to lock it into the sequence.

---

## 3. Post-Production Handover

### .OTIO Export
1.  Once your sequence is approved, click the **EXPORT** button in the top header.
2.  Choose **OpenTimelineIO (.otio)**.
3.  This file can be imported directly into **DaVinci Resolve**, **Premiere Pro**, or **Final Cut Pro**.
4.  The export includes all media references, AI prompts, and scene metadata.

---

## 4. BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Settings
Configure your AI engines in the **Settings** panel (Gear icon):
- **Google AI Studio**: Required for Script Breakdown, Moodboard, and Prompt Optimization.
- **SiliconFlow / Runway / Seedance**: Required for high-fidelity video generation.
